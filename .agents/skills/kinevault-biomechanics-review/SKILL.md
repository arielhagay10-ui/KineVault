# Biomechanics review

Use for exercise classification, taxonomy, movement descriptions, or biomechanics logic. Follow `BIOMECHANICS.md` as the authority.

- Muscles, joint actions, movement patterns, and rig poses are distinct concepts.
- Resistance profile means external mechanical challenge across range of motion; do not substitute muscle-length bias.
- Machine, setup, and cable geometry can change resistance profile.
- Use `unknown` when evidence is insufficient. Make no unsupported medical or rehabilitation claims.
