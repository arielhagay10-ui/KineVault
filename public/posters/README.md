# Home anatomy poster

`home-anatomy.webp` is an original KineVault capture of `defaultScene` in
`src/lib/motion/workshop.ts`, at 1467 ms with the deltoid group highlighted.
Captured 3 October 2026. No exercise or user content was used.

The WebP is 623 × 500 pixels and 7,770 bytes. Encode the original PNG with
FFmpeg's `libwebp`, quality 82. The local capture is retained under
`.local-artifacts/efficiency-review/home-default-scene.png`.

The anatomy is adapted from Z-Anatomy by Gauthier Kervyn and contributors,
licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, and BodyParts3D © The Database Center for Life Science,
licensed CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. Preserve the credits displayed beside the poster.
Full source notices and license links are in
`public/models/z-anatomy/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
